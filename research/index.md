# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

We study the rules that govern RNA processing and what happens when those rules are disrupted. Our work connects computational prediction with experimental testing.

- **Splicing prediction and regulation:** Develop models to predict splice outcomes and identify the sequences and regulatory factors that influence them.
- **Genetic variation and disease:** Combine RNA sequencing with other molecular data to study splicing changes in cancer and neurodegenerative disease.
- **Methods and assays:** Develop high-throughput experiments and software to quantify and visualize splicing across tissues and conditions.
